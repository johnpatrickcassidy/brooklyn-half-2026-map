Street Closure Transition Animations

Explore three different animation styles for when street closures become active or inactive as you move the time slider. Click "Trigger" to see the animation in action on the sample lines.

Direction 1: Grow & Fade

Smooth and organic. The line scales up from the center while fading in when closing, and fades out smoothly when opening back up.

Direction 2: Paint-on Trace

Dynamic and precise. The line appears to draw itself from start to finish along the road network when the closure becomes active.

Direction 3: Warning Flash

High visibility alert. The line flashes rapidly between yellow and red when it first appears to immediately command attention.
