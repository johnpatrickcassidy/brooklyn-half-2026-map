Density Ridgeline

Option 2: Mile-Marker Density Bars

Abstracts the route into a clean, horizontal 1D track (Mile 0 to 13.1). Renders a live computational density histogram showing the aggregate concentration of runners within each 0.5-mile bin as time progresses.

Trade-offs:
  • Clearly exposes congestion points and runner pack spreading
  • Highly performant; trivial geometric computation overhead
  • Sacrifices physical street-level spatial orientation
Macro Load Stack

Option 3: Stacked Macro Flow Area

A macroscopic systems dashboard plotting cumulative runner states against time. Shows the phase shifts between "Active on Course" volume and cumulative "Finished" status to evaluate overall system exhaustion.

Trade-offs:
  • Perfect for high-level executive scheduling and timing limits
  • Unbeatable rendering speeds (renders a single pre-computed vector path)
  • Zero geographic or micro-level position awareness
