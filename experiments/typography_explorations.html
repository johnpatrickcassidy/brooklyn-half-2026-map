Typography & Layout Directions

Explaining 5 ways to present the closure data, prioritizing the active time window and street name above all secondary details.

6:00 AM - 11:30 AM Flatbush Avenue

Direction 1: Time Dominant

Places the active time window as the absolute hero element in huge red typography, followed by a bold uppercase street name. All secondary data is omitted.

Direction 2: Ticket Stub

Uses a split race-bib style container. The left side locks the time in a heavy black block, while the right side holds the street and a small muted segment description.

6:00 AM - 11:30 AM FLATBUSH AVE

Direction 3: Minimal Grid

A clean, two-column data row. Removes all cards and padding to create a highly efficient line-item readout. Perfect for dense lists.

Closed from 6:00 AM to 11:30 AM on Flatbush Avenue.

Direction 4: Narrative Sentence

Translates the structured data into a bold, natural-language sentence. Highly human-readable and breaks away from standard label/value forms.

6:00 AM - 11:30 AM Flatbush Avenue No Parking: Fri 4:00 PM - Sat 11:30 AM

Direction 5: Dark Alert Card

Uses full dark mode matching the flyer header. Features bright yellow time text and keeps the secondary no-parking data in tiny, low-contrast text at the bottom.
